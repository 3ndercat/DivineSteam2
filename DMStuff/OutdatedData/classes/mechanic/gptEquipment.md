Design Advice

To keep the class balanced and distinct from the Artificer, consider these principles:

Most inventions should grant one clear benefit, rather than multiple strong effects.
Stronger inventions should cost more Materia and occupy valuable body slots.
Favor at-will passive effects and once-per-short-rest gadgets over effects that replicate spells.
Make body-slot competition meaningful. For example, if both the Grapple Launcher and Arc Cannon use the Right Arm slot, the player has to choose mobility or offense for that adventure.

This approach encourages players to customize their Mechanic's "loadout" each day, giving the class a distinct identity as an adaptable inventor rather than a spellcaster with gadgets.
